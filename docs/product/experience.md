# Product experience

The Solvay 1927 route is handcrafted, fact-constrained content. QuillForge is the generative Project Two workshop and does not replace or rewrite the Solvay route.

In the VR laboratory, interact with the standalone female researcher below the portal. Her first menu is exactly:

- 体验现有世界
- 世界编织
- 暂时离开

`世界编织` continues through theme and role selection, opens QuillForge inside Echo, generates a worldbook, presents it for review, and starts the GALGAME only after confirmation. The Echo chrome provides retry and 返回实验室. Escape delegates to Echo's system menu; save, load, continue, return-title and exit remain host-game actions.
